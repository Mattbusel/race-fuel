Race Fuel builds your race-day nutrition plan from your own numbers, then reminds you to take every gel.

Race day nutrition fails in the details: the right grams at the right time, from products you have actually trained with. Race Fuel does the arithmetic and carries the plan on the start line.

ATHLETE
• Sweat rate from a weigh-in test, corrected for race-day heat
• Sodium loss per hour from your sweat saltiness or a measured value
• Carb targets from your gut training, bike at the full rate and run at 80%
• Fluid capped at what a gut absorbs

RACE
• Presets from sprint triathlon to full iron, marathon, half, century, gran fondo and 50 km trail, or your own legs
• Per-leg carb, fluid and sodium targets, start time and expected finish

PRODUCTS AND PLAN
• Your gels, chews, bottles as you mix them, salt capsules, bars, cola and aid-station cups
• Auto-fill to the targets, then adjust counts per leg with live gauges
• Caffeine budget against your body weight, shopping list

RACE DAY
• A timeline of every intake by clock time
